### Introduction

**Joiefull** is an intuitive and elegant fashion application built entirely with SwiftUI.
It allows users to browse a catalog of clothing items organized by categories, explore detailed product views, and manage their favorite pieces effortlessly.

---

### Features
- Browse clothing items by category
- View detailed product information and ratings
- Add or remove favorites
- See how popular each item is
- Share their impressions and discoveries

---

### Architecture

Joiefull follows the MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel) architecture to ensure a clean and modular codebase.
